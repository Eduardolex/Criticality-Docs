# What do I mean by Criticality?

By criticality what I mean is when matter is at the edge of order and chaos, an example being stars; they are essentially nuclear reactors and they compose several elements with new organizational properties that you can't 
reduce to it's previous states of matter: Helium, Carbon, Oxygen etc...
