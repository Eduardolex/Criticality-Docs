# How is value ontologically real?


Lets take for example truth, beauty and the good. Most people probably have them categorized as epistemically subjective human phenomenon, but in actuality they are ontologically valid features of the cosmos.
For example lets take the color red, one can argue that red doesn't objectively exist in the cosmos and is just our perceptual structures interacting with wavelengths in order to percieve the world. But the color red just as it's made of relational structures(wave lengths)
It still has causal influence in the cosmos regardless of our perception, therefore red is real in an ontological sense; just like red is real truth, beauty and the good are ontologically valid structures that have relational patterns that influence the cosmos regardless of our perception of it.
